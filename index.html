Our Heritage

Our Story

Long ago, Tavga Hill was a beloved gathering spot near Sarchnar where families and young people came together to share meals, spend unhurried afternoons, and enjoy the cool water of an active waterfall.

Over time, the waterfall dried up and the hillside grew quiet. Today, Tavga Hill is lovingly revived as a safe, welcoming, multi-generation picnic destination right inside Slemani.

We purposely kept the original weathered stone walls just as they are—preserving the authentic story, timeless stonework, and cherished character of the hill for every generation to enjoy.

Hillside Amenities

What's Waiting For You

Every terrace and pathway has been carefully equipped so you can relax and focus on your time together.

♿

The "Stramp" Path

Our custom zigzag stairs-and-ramp hybrid path makes walking up the hillside smooth and friendly for both strollers and wheelchairs.

🥐

Stone Corner Café & Bakery

Built right into the historic stone kiosk halfway up the hill, serving warm coffee and freshly baked goods in a cozy stone setting.

🏪

Street-Level Essentials Kiosk

Located right at the bottom of the hill so you can easily pick up charcoal, picnic essentials, snacks, and other goods on arrival.

⛺

All-Weather Wooden Gazebos

Two wooden picnic gazebos sit on every terrace, each fitted with roll-down clear rain curtains to shelter your family from wind and rain.

⛲

Lush, Mud-Free Green Terraces

Enjoy fresh grass, vibrant greenery, clean stepping stones, and a soothing water fountain—with zero mud underfoot.

🛡️

Child-Safe Wooden Fencing

A sturdy 1-metre-high wooden fence runs along every terrace edge to keep children safe while they explore and play.

🔥

Built-In Stainless Steel Grills

Space-saving stainless steel barbecue grills are mounted along the fence, complete with a steel heat shield behind each grill for safe cooking.

🚰

Terrace Wash Taps & Ash Bins

Every single terrace includes its own stone wash tap with running water and a dedicated ash bin for effortless cleanup.

💨

Hilltop Open-Air Shisha Zone

Positioned at the very top of the hill so guests can unwind in the breeze while smoke never reaches the family terraces below.

🐿️

Squirrel Feeding Stations

Friendly squirrels live on the hill with refillable feeding stations so children and adults alike can enjoy watching and feeding the animals.

✨

Warm Evening Lighting

Restored lamp posts, warm LED pathway lights, and waterproof LED strips tucked under the fences and gazebos create a magical evening glow.

⛑️

Restrooms, First Aid & Safety

Clean restrooms for men and women, accessible first aid kits, and a clearly marked assembly point give your family complete peace of mind.

Easy & Relaxing

Your Visit, Step by Step

Everything at Tavga Hill is arranged so you can settle in quickly and enjoy your day at your own pace.

1

Arrive at the Front of the Hill

Meet your family and friends right at the street-level entrance near Sarchnar, where warm lighting and clear signage welcome you in.

2

Pick Up Your Essentials at the Kiosk

Stop by the street-level kiosk to grab charcoal, picnic supplies, snacks, and anything else you need for your outing.

3

Walk Up the "Stramp"

Stroll comfortably up our zigzag stairs-and-ramp path, specially built so strollers, toddlers, and grandparents can ascend with ease.

4

Choose Your Terrace, Gazebo & Grill

Settle into your favorite green terrace complete with a wooden gazebo, stainless steel barbecue grill, running water tap, and ash bin.

5

Visit the Café or Hilltop Shisha Lounge

Take a relaxing coffee and bakery break halfway up at Stone Corner Café, or head to the very top of the hill for the open-air shisha lounge.

🧺

Good to know: Tavga Hill is designed as a mostly self-service picnic destination, giving your family the freedom to grill, dine, and relax just the way you like.
